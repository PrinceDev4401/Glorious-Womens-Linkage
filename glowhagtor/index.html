GLOWHAGTOR Head

About the Department

Miss Ernestina Oforiwaa Koduah is the Head of Glowhagtor, a department under Glorious Women’s Linkage. She is a professional teacher, mentor, nurturer, and entrepreneur with a strong passion for girl-child education and leadership development.

As Head of Glowhagtor, Ernestina provides leadership and direction for the department’s activities. We work to inspire leadership in young girls by promoting access to education, mentorship, and empowerment, particularly within underserved and rural communities.

We believe education is key to raising confident, purpose-driven female pioneers. Through our programs, we educate and empower young girls to pursue their academic aspirations and become whatever they desire to be, while also raising awareness on issues affecting the girl child.

This department’s activities are geared towards educating girls and empowering them to pursue their academics and become whatever they desire to be. Our activities also include educating the general public on issues affecting the girl child and women.

Who We Support

At the start of each year, we hold a talk show on education and social issues relating to girls in a selected town or village — and offer support to girls in these four categories.

Out-of-School Girls

Girls who have dropped out of school or cannot attend due to lack of finances.

Financial Support

Girls who are in school but need extra financial support to keep going.

Mentorship

Girls who have financial support but need mentors to guide them academically.

Vocational Training

Girls who need help to learn a trade and build a livelihood.

What We Do Throughout the Year

GLOWHAGTOR walks with the girls we support all year round, providing what they need based on their category.

Annual Community Talk Show

A talk show on education and social issues relating to girls, held in a selected town or village at the beginning of each year, where girls are identified for support.

Parent & Public Forums

Forums that educate parents and the public on giving girls equal opportunities as boys — at home, in school, and in the community.

Quarterly Educational Shows

Educational shows hosted in selected schools and communities to shed light on issues affecting girls and women in all spheres of life.

Help Us Keep Girls in School
