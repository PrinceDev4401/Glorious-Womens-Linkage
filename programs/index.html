What We Do

Our Programs

Conferences, mentorship, outreaches, and empowerment initiatives — follow the latest from GLOWLINK's work across Ghana and beyond.

Ongoing Programmes

Regular activities that keep our members growing all year round.

Weekly Member Activities

Bible study and prayer on Thursdays and Saturday presentations on education, health, entrepreneurship, self-confidence, marriage, and parenting — plus daily devotions to start each day on a good note.

Annual & Regional Conferences

With members all over the country, annual regional conferences enable members to meet physically, get acquainted, and be empowered with leadership skills they use to mentor other young ladies and girls.

Media Engagements

Featured on talk shows across Ghana — Y FM, Orange FM, Okay FM, Hello FM, Sharp FM, Spirit FM, and Kessben TV — speaking on teenage pregnancy, self-identity, and igniting leadership skills in women.

News & Highlights

Stories from our programs and outreaches over the years.

May 2022

GLOBE4HER Summit Brings Global Voices to Ghana

The GLOBE4HER Summit featured speakers including Dr. Joyce Aryee, Mrs. Comfort Ocran, Madam Catherine Krobo Edusie, and Ms. Valarie Waswa — a Kenyan lawyer — educating girls and women on the importance of making impact globally.

Achievements
2025

2025 Sponsorship & Partnership Achievements

GLOWLINK welcomed generous support this year from Twellium Industries, Promasidor Ghana, Imprexion Eye Clinic, Blueskies Company, and individual donors — fueling our mentorship and menstrual education outreaches across the Kpone and Akuapem Districts.

Sponsorship
2025

Twellium Industries Limited

Supported GLOWHAGTOR's mentorship and donation outreach at Okushibri (Kpone District) on 15th July, and GLOWLEAD's menstrual education at Adenya M/A Basic School (Akuapem District) on 20th July, with 50 packs of Verna Mineral Water.

Sponsorship
2025

Promasidor Ghana Limited

Supported both GLOWHAGTOR and GLOWLEAD with 10 boxes of assorted Cowbell products for their outreach programmes.

Sponsorship
2025

Imprexion Eye Clinic

Supported GLOWLEAD's Menstrual Education programme with a donation of 5 boxes of sanitary pads.

New Partner
2025

Blueskies Company

Joined GLOWLINK as a new sponsor, supporting GLOWLEAD's Menstrual Education programme.

Mentorship
2024

Girls' Education Mentorship at Saviepe Community

GLOWHAGTOR partnered with GLOWLEAD, NCCE, and GES for a one-day outreach in Ho West District — mentoring students, engaging parents on girls' education, and hosting a nurse-led menstrual hygiene session.

Advocacy
2025

Menstrual Hygiene Advocacy at Adenya M/A Basic School

GLOWLEAD organized an advocacy event in the Eastern Region to promote menstrual literacy, equipping young girls with the knowledge and resources to manage their health with confidence.

Advocacy
2026

Menstrual Hygiene Advocacy Outreach at AKODE Basic School

GLOWLEAD reached 157 students and 11 teachers at AKODE Basic School, Somanya — combining menstrual hygiene education, item donations, and a Pad Bank contribution, while also flagging needy students for further support.

Conference
January 2022

First Annual Conference Empowers Women Globally

Our first annual conference was held to empower women globally about their potential — a great success with global speakers on board.

Advocacy
2022

Mentorship & Menstrual Advocacy at Aseseeso

A mentorship programme with menstrual advocacy and donations in the Okere District, Eastern Region — educating girls, parents, and the youth, including awareness on the dangers of drug abuse.

Outreach
2022

Support for Accra Psychiatric Hospital

Donation of sanitary items, foodstuffs, and toiletries to support people with mental illness, with worship and prayers for their recovery.

Education
August 2021

Menstrual Education Reaches Ten Schools in Jakobu

Girls from ten schools in the Ashanti Region were educated on the use of sanitary pads and healthy menstrual practices, alongside a talk on the importance of education.

Outreach
November 2021

Elderly Care at Tabitha Aged Care Center

Donated food items and toiletries to one hundred aged men and women at Afiama and shared the word of God with them.

Conference
2020

Virtual Empowerment During COVID-19

When the pandemic halted physical activities, GLOWLINK moved programmes online — hosting virtual conferences with globally invited speakers to help members and women overcome fear and panic.

Outreach
2019

Nsawam Prison Donation Supports 400 Inmates

Supported four hundred prisoners with basic needs and provided healthcare products for female inmates — a partnership effort that continues today.

Education
2018

2,000 Girls Return to School

Educational seminars helped two thousand girls return to school, alongside vocational training for one hundred and fifty parents of early-marriage victims in the Northern Region.

Health
2018

Health Outreach Screens 300+ Women in Nima

Community education on sanitation and hygienic living, with free screening of over three hundred women for hepatitis B, diabetes, and breast cancer.

Education
2017

School Outreach in the Ashanti Region

Three hundred girls at Anglican Junior High School and Riverside Academy learned effective study habits and how to overcome low self-esteem, followed by donations of books and stationery.

Mentorship
2016

Mentorship Programme Launches in Cape Coast

One hundred students in the Central Region were educated on the importance of self-identity and how to achieve excellent results.

Outreach
2016

Street Donations at Tema Station

Sanitary products and food items donated to head porters in Accra, with specialists encouraging them to pursue their dreams irrespective of gender.
