Meet Our CEO & President

The visionary leadership behind Glorious Women’s Linkage (GLOWLINK) and its mission to empower girls and women across Africa and beyond.

Mrs. Rosemond Köhler

CEO / President — GLOWLINK
YALI Member Global Woman Club WISE Community UN Women Global TEF Guild Mentor President, ACEA Ghana

Mrs. Rosemond Köhler is the CEO/President of Glorious Women’s Linkage (GLOWLINK). A global leader, mentor to women, philanthropist, and author, she is an Alumna of the African Civic Engagement Academy (ACEA) and now serves as President of ACEA Ghana.

Rosemond is a voice for the voiceless women in society, with respect and dignity for all people. She desires to work for a better world — one where women discover themselves, live empowered lives, and lift others as they rise.

Education & Values

Academic Background

Holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Management Studies from the University of Cape Coast, and a Master’s Degree in Insurance and Business Continuity from the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, specializing in disaster recovery and strategic planning that helps organizations minimize loss and disruption.

Core Values

Focused on achieving her goals, she believes in integrity, consistency, persistence, and resilience. A responsible and organized leader with a high capacity for teamwork, she adjusts easily to change and works well under pressure — dedicated to developing the organization with experience and integrity.

Her Passion

She yearns to see the youth become responsible citizens, nation builders, and world changers — passionate about seeing them contribute their quota to the development of the nation and the world. She is a revivalist to humanity and to women in general.

Projects of GLOWLINK & Their Impact

GLOWLINK focuses on mentoring girls and women, educating them to press for progress in every endeavour of their lives, occupy their places in life, and stay focused in the pursuit of their goals. From its establishment to date, the organization has mentored five thousand (5,000) girls and women, transforming the lives of many people — including its own members.

2016

Mentorship Programme — Cape Coast

Organized a mentorship programme in the Central Region of Ghana to educate one hundred (100) students on the importance of self-identity and how to achieve excellent results.

Street Donations — Tema Station, Accra

Donated sanitary products and food items to head potters, and invited specialists to encourage them to pursue their dreams irrespective of gender, alongside education on sanitation.

2017

School Outreach — Ashanti Region

Visited Anglican Junior High School and Riverside Academy to educate three hundred (300) girls on effective study habits and overcoming low self-esteem, followed by donations of exercise books, textbooks, and stationery.

2018

Back-to-School & Anti-Early-Marriage Campaign

Helped two thousand (2,000) girls return to school through educational seminars, and held an empowerment outreach in the Northern Region — including vocational training for one hundred and fifty (150) parents of early-marriage victims to give them financial independence.

Entrepreneurship Training — University of Cape Coast

Trained one thousand (1,000) widows, single mothers, and youths with income-generating skills to reduce unemployment and social vices.

Health Outreach — Nima, Accra

Educated the community on sanitation and hygienic living, and screened over three hundred (300) women for hepatitis B, diabetes, and breast cancer.

Orphanage & Prison Visits

Dined with one hundred (100) orphans at New Life Orphanage in Nungua, and visited Ankaful Prison in Cape Coast to dine with the prisoners and share the word of God (November 2018).

2019

Nsawam Prison Donation

Supported four hundred (400) prisoners with basic needs and provided healthcare products for female inmates. GLOWLINK continues to seek support from pharmaceutical companies and individuals to supply needed medications to prisoners and other less-privileged people.

2020

Virtual Empowerment During COVID-19

When the pandemic halted physical activities, GLOWLINK innovated by moving programmes online — hosting virtual conferences with globally invited speakers to help members and women overcome fear and panic.

2021

Menstrual Education — Jakobu Community, Ashanti Region

Educated girls from ten (10) schools on the use of sanitary pads and healthy menstrual practices, alongside a talk on the importance of education (August 2021).

Elderly Care — Tabitha Aged Care Center, Afiama

Donated food items and toiletries to one hundred (100) aged men and women and shared the word of God with them (November 2021).

2022

First Annual Conference

Held in January 2022 to empower women globally about their potential — a great success with global speakers on board.

GLOBE4HER Summit

Held in May 2022 with speakers including Dr. Joyce Aryee, Mrs. Comfort Ocran, Madam Catherine Krobo Edusie, and Ms. Valarie Waswa (a Kenyan lawyer), educating girls and women on the importance of making impact globally.

Mentorship & Menstrual Advocacy — Aseseeso

A mentorship programme with menstrual advocacy and donations at Aseseeso in the Okere District, Eastern Region — educating girls, parents, and the youth, including awareness on the dangers of drug abuse rising in the community.

Accra Psychiatric Hospital Donation

Donation of sanitary items, foodstuffs, and toiletries to support people with mental illness, with worship and prayers for their recovery.

Media & Ongoing Programmes

Media Engagements

GLOWLINK has featured on talk shows across Ghana — including Y FM, Orange FM, Okay FM, Hello FM, Sharp FM, Spirit FM, and Kessben TV — speaking on teenage pregnancy, self-identity, the temperament of women and girls, and igniting leadership skills in women, using every opportunity to educate the public.

Regional Conferences

With members all over the country, annual regional conferences enable members to meet physically in their regions, get acquainted, and be empowered with leadership skills they use to mentor other young ladies and girls.

Weekly Member Activities

Members are taken through Bible study and prayer on Tuesdays and Thursdays, led by groups on a rotating schedule to meet their spiritual needs. Saturdays feature presentations by members or invited guests on education, health, entrepreneurship, self-confidence, marriage, parenting, and more. On Sundays, members share what they learned at church, and daily devotions are shared to help members start their day on a good note.

“I aspire to see GLOWLINK reach out to as many people as possible all over the world — recruiting country directors across Africa so that many girls, women, and even men can benefit from the impact we are making in the lives of people.”
